## When to Seek Repair
If the printer reports a persistent hardware error, makes unusual mechanical noises, smells hot, or repeatedly jams after safe checks, stop forcing print jobs. Consult the model’s manual or an authorized repair provider. Disconnect power if the manual advises it and never open high-voltage or heated internal assemblies.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Workflow
1. Confirm the exact Brother model and its supported connection types.
2. Check power, control-panel alerts, paper, and supplies.
3. Produce a built-in status or test page if the model allows it.
4. Check USB or network connectivity as appropriate.
5. Confirm that the operating system sees the correct printer.
6. Clear only stuck jobs and retry a short test print.
7. Verify the driver against the official Brother support listing.
8. Test scanning separately if the device is multifunction.
9. Escalate persistent hardware warnings to qualified support.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Do I need a CD to install a Brother printer?
Usually not. For supported models, the appropriate driver or setup package can be downloaded from official Brother support.

### Why is my Brother printer not detected?
A cable problem, wrong network, unsupported connection method, driver issue, or a device error can prevent detection.

### Can I connect every Brother printer to Wi-Fi?
No. Wireless capability depends on the exact model; check its specifications.

### Why does the printer show offline even when powered on?
The computer may be using an outdated connection, paused queue, or unreachable network address.

### How do I choose the right driver?
Match the complete model number and operating-system version to the official driver listing.

### Can I install it on a Mac?
Many Brother models support macOS, but compatibility and setup methods vary by model and OS release.

### Should I reset the printer first?
No. Try non-destructive checks first; a reset may erase network settings and other preferences.

### What should I do after installation?
Print a test page and, on multifunction models, test scanning separately if needed.

### Can I use a USB hub?
Direct USB connection is preferable while troubleshooting; some hubs cause power or communication issues.

### Is independent online guidance official Brother support?
No. A third-party guide is independent unless it explicitly belongs to the manufacturer.
